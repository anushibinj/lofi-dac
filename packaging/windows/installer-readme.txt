Lofify turns everything your computer plays into lofi.

BEFORE YOU START: Lofify needs a virtual audio cable on Windows. This installer does not include
one (its licence does not allow redistribution) and never changes your audio settings.

  1. Install VB-CABLE from https://vb-audio.com/Cable/ (run its setup as administrator, reboot).
  2. Set "CABLE Input" as your default playback device in Windows Sound settings.
  3. Start Lofify; the first-run wizard walks you through choosing your real speakers.

Important: when Lofify is not running you will hear nothing until you switch the default
playback device back to your speakers. The guide is in docs\SETUP_WINDOWS.md after installation.
